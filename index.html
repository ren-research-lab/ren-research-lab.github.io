Welcome to the PURE Lab @ UAlberta!
The Precision Utilization of Resources and Energy (PURE) Lab at the University of Alberta develops electrochemical technologies for the precise conversion, recovery, and manufacturing of sustainable materials. We integrate extractive metallurgy, electrochemistry, materials chemistry, reactor engineering, and process analysis to control how electrons, ions, and molecules move through electrified interfaces and reactors. By connecting molecular-level mechanisms with device- and system-scale design, we aim to enable sustainable critical mineral recovery, low-carbon chemical production, and industrial decarbonization.
