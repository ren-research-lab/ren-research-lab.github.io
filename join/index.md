## Graduate Student Positions

We welcome inquiries from motivated students interested in electrochemistry and related research areas.

Prospective M.Sc. and Ph.D. students must submit a formal application through the Department of Chemical and Materials Engineering at the University of Alberta. Details regarding admission requirements, application procedures, and deadlines are available on the [department’s graduate studies website](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/engineering/chemical-materials-engineering/graduate-studies/index.html).

Students interested in joining the group are encouraged to contact Prof. Ren before submitting their formal application. Please include the following materials in your email:

- A brief introduction describing your research background and interests
- A short explanation of how your experience and goals align with the group’s research
- An up-to-date CV
- Unofficial academic transcripts
- Representative publications, presentations, or other research outputs, if available
- Names and contact information for at least two academic references, particularly for Ph.D. applicants

Please use **`[Graduate Applicant]`** in the email subject line.

## Undergraduate Student Positions

Undergraduate students interested in research courses, co-op placements, summer projects, or other research opportunities are welcome to contact Prof. Ren.

Please include the following information in your email:

- A brief introduction
- Your degree program and current year of study
- Relevant coursework, laboratory skills, or research experience
- Your expected availability
- A current CV
- An unofficial academic transcript

Students are also encouraged to explore funding opportunities offered through the University of Alberta’s [Undergraduate Research Initiative](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/current-students/undergraduate-research-initiative/funding/index.html).

Please use **`[Undergraduate Applicant]`** in the email subject line.
